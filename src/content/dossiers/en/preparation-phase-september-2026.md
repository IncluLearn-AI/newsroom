The funded IncluLearn.AI project period starts on **1 October 2026**. A substantial set of foundations was prepared in September so that the project would not start from zero.

The work summarised here is therefore explicitly documented as a **preparation phase**. Posts are placed according to the actual event period even though the newsroom entries are published retrospectively.

The main areas were project organisation, technical infrastructure, collaboration and communication, AI governance, requirements analysis, the scientific and technical knowledge base, the evolutionary vision prototype, and the preparation of modular processing services.

Each post in this dossier groups several related changes into one editorial event. Individual commits or high activity counts are not presented as progress on their own.
