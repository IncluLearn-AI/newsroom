## A living knowledge base rather than a static literature store

The scientific and technical state of the art was prepared as a living review. It includes a literature matrix, a machine-readable literature index, search and update strategies, research questions and documented research gaps.

The structure is intended to do more than collect existing methods and tools. It should make clear which findings feed into requirements, technical architecture and later experiments.

## Benchmark success is not user success

An important methodological principle separates technical metrics from actual usefulness for blind and visually impaired learners. A strong model result on a benchmark does not by itself show that a generated representation is understandable or educationally appropriate.

Annotation and reference logic, together with experimental protocols, were therefore prepared for semantic evaluation. Model adaptation and fine-tuning remain possible research options rather than being assumed as the default path.

## Context

The research base is designed for continuous updating. Technical claims about AI models, benchmarks and tools can age quickly, so source status and date are intended to remain visible.
