## What was prepared?

IncluLearn.AI received a central, versioned working and knowledge base that brings together project goals, work packages, milestones, roles, decisions and project-wide rules.

Principles for accessible collaboration, AI assistance and the handling of sensitive data were prepared in parallel. Important project information is intended to be documented text-first wherever possible, while subject-matter and accessibility review remain human responsibilities.

## Preparing WP1

A concrete start plan for the first weeks of the first work package was also prepared and linked to central project steering. This makes it possible to distinguish preparatory work from activities that will take place during the funded project period.

## Context

These activities took place before the funded project period. They are organisational and methodological preparations and are not presented as completed project milestones.
