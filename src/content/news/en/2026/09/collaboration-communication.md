A community structure for GitHub Discussions was prepared for asynchronous exchange. It is intended to support open questions, ideas, subject-matter discussion and communication with partners.

At the same time, Discussions were explicitly defined as **not** being the authoritative source for tasks or durable project knowledge. Decisions, requirements and concrete tasks are transferred into the appropriate versioned project structures.

In parallel, an editorial working base was created for branding, press, social media, events and transfer. In simplified form, the intended path is: **discuss → develop editorially → review → publish**.

The first logo proposals were also prepared with textual alternatives. Accessibility is therefore considered in communication work rather than being postponed until the later public website.
