## Governance as part of the technical work

IncluLearn.AI is intended not only to use AI but also to make that use traceable. A dedicated governance structure was therefore prepared before the project start.

It covers principles for AI transparency and human review, working rules for data protection and security, risk and compliance registers, system and agent cards, and machine-readable controls. A provenance schema was added so that the origin and review status of results can later be traced.

## Human approval remains a separate step

A central principle is that AI systems and agents must not classify their own output as verified or approved. Automation may prepare, support checking and structure work, while subject-matter approval remains separate.

## Data classification for personal output profiles

Personal accessibility and output profiles were then included in the data-classification work. This anticipates that personalised accessibility is functionally important while the corresponding profile data must not be treated like ordinary application data.

## Context

The governance framework is a project-specific working basis and does not replace binding THM policies or review by responsible institutional functions. Its role is to support research and development while keeping review, responsibility and data provenance visible.
